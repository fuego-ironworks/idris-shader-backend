# Checked coordinate pairing

Control envelope: https://github.com/isomorphisms/flexible-pipes/issues/116.

The shader interface now receives one vector-coordinate packet and one
covector-coordinate packet in one named real Cartesian frame. Dimension,
dual role and equal-dimension space identity are checked before lowering.
`coordinate_pairing` approximates evaluation of the covector on the vector;
the separate grayscale entry maps its scalar result to RGBA.

The numerical interface is `Shader.Coordinates`: it does not redefine the
mathematical owner in Idriç's `MathematicalSpaces`/`EuclideanGeometry` library.
`CartesianReal32` corresponds to the named `real32_space`; the other 32D frame
belongs to `other_real32_space`. These are standard bases of distinct real
spaces, not two implicit interchangeable bases. A general basis conversion
needs a separately checked conversion contract.

The first storage implementation uses fixed scalar arrays of length 4 or 32.
Eight four-component chunks may be a future qualified layout of the same
32D object; packed width cannot change its mathematical dimension. The old
sixteen-uniform examples and names have no permanent compatibility aliases.

Ordinary source shader carriers are now `ShaderFloatTuple`, with GLSL's vecN
spelling retained at emission. A tuple alone supplies no vector-space, dual,
point, complex, colour or physical-register role. Source signatures are
recorded in stable `checked-shader-source-v1` sidecars before role erasure.
The numerical source primitives use component names: `add_components`,
`subtract_components`, `scale_components`, `component_product_sum`,
`euclidean_component_norm` and `normalize_components_unchecked`. These are
distinct from mathematical vector-space and metric operations.

The IR preserves the distinction through `TFloatTuple`, `AFloatTuple`,
`SomeFloatTuple` and component-operation constructors, including
`RComponentProductSum` and `RNormalizeComponentsUnchecked`. The GLSL and mock
emitters consume those constructors directly; old vector-operation aliases are
absent. Target-language `vecN` and mock machine opcodes retain their target role.

Canonical qualified declarations identify shader types and primitives.
Unrelated declarations with matching short names are rejected. A backend
error now produces a nonzero process status; error text is not a success.

Local evidence at the recorded material source: checked source, 4D/32D
emission, five role/space/dimension/spoof refusals, retained backend regression
checks, all six Mesa shader/link and framebuffer samples, 1024 exact basis
readbacks and two coordinate-change samples on an RGBA32F target. The Idriç
checker in ai-ci independently agrees and rejects an executed one-operand
coordinate-swap mutant and an omitted-case result. Mesa llvmpipe is software
host execution, not a physical PowerVR/Mali receipt or a mathematical proof.

rotation, application/ABI and physical-target migrations remain incomplete;
